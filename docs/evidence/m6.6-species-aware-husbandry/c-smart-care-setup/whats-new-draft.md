# What's New draft — not released

## Set up care at your own pace

Open **Smart care setup** from an Animal's Care page to review available care references,
choose the context that fits your animal, and adjust a reminder. You'll see the exact
change before you confirm it. Conflicting sources stay visible, and manual setup remains
available when reviewed guidance is missing or cannot supply a suitable interval.

Confirmed reminders use the existing Care, Today and Calendar views. Recording care
updates the next due date. Existing schedules stay under your control; nothing is added
or changed just by viewing a guide or opening setup.

You don't need to change your current schedules. This is an optional setup path.
