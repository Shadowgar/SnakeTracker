# Development draft — Frog tracking

This is development-only wording, not a confirmed website release or production announcement.

## Track your Frogs alongside the rest of your collection

Choose Amphibian when adding an animal, save its species and photo, and keep its notes and
care history in the same familiar collection as your Snakes, Spiders, Lizards and Scorpions.

Record what you offered and the feeding outcome, weight and length measurements, enclosure
moves and spending. Use shared Inventory, view your recorded trends, and choose the reminders
that fit your own routine. Linked species remain available in Guides & Species Reference.

Initial Frog tracking includes feeding, weight and length. Species reference stays separate
from captive-care advice, and Care Keeper does not choose a diet, schedule or growth target
for you. Specialized life stages and researched Amphibian Care Guides are future work.
