## Care for the habitat and each plant

Mark an enclosure as bioactive and keep its plants and maintenance together, even when an animal moves to another home. Set a separate watering schedule for each plant entry, review it, and confirm. Plant watering, enclosure cleaning and water changes remain separate from your animals' feeding and measurements.

Confirmed tasks appear in Today, Calendar and reminders. Record watering from the plant or its task, and correct, void or reinstate records through maintenance history. Due dates follow the effective records. Removing a plant stops its future tasks while retaining its history.

You choose the intervals. Missing reference guidance does not block setup, and bioactive mode does not automatically change cleaning or watering schedules. Your existing enclosures keep their current mode and schedules; no action is required.
