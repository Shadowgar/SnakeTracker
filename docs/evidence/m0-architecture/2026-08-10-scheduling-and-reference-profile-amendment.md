# Scheduling and Husbandry Reference Profile Amendment

Accepted: August 10, 2026

The owner approved ADR-0038 as the narrow architecture-freeze amendment governing M5
effective-history reminder scheduling and M6 analytics/reference-profile extension points.

The amendment preserves Animal and Enclosure events as authoritative husbandry facts, Reminder Rule
as the scheduling aggregate, reminder facts and analytics as rebuildable consumers, and owner
configuration as authoritative. It does not authorize M6 implementation, species advice without
curated sources, or the deferred AI assistant.
