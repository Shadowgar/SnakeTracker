# Trusted Local Demo-Household Amendment

Accepted: August 16, 2026

The owner approved [ADR-0040](../../adr/0040-trusted-local-demo-household-provisioning.md) as a
narrow architecture-freeze amendment for the M6 local owner-review environment. It permits one
reserved, deterministic fictional household to be created through canonical household events and
the current authorization projection in an explicitly allow-listed local environment.

The approval does not authorize browser/API household creation, invitations, general household
administration, direct relational demo seeding, production behavior changes, or mutation of the
owner's existing household and history. Bidirectional household isolation and one-instance
qualification on port 8081 remain release-blocking M6 correction evidence.
