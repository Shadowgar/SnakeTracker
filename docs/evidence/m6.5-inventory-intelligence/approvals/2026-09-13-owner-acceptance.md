# M6.5 Owner Acceptance

Status: **M6.5 Inventory Intelligence and Cost Tracking accepted**

Accepted: **September 13, 2026**

The owner accepts the complete M6.5 milestone: ADR-0042, structured Inventory and
Inventory-authoritative Feeding, unified acquisitions and FIFO costing, Inventory intelligence and
physical counts, visual-first financial reports, the decimal-weight correction, and the final
technical qualification represented by PRs #10 through #13.

Acceptance is grounded in the [final M6.5 qualification](../final-qualification/README.md), including
the authoritative 619-test quality gate, migration and replay compatibility, isolated backup and
restore rehearsal, live-data integrity proof, household authorization, responsive browser and
accessibility qualification, and healthy native ARM64 Raspberry Pi runtime.

This acceptance authorizes final integration of M6.5. It does not qualify formal M7 Raspberry Pi
deployment and recovery, accept M8 release qualification, enable M9 public sharing, or define an
additional milestone that is not present in the approved roadmap.
